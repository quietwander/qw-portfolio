# Trademarks

"QW Portfolio" and the QW Portfolio icon are trademarks of Quiet Wander. They are not covered by the code licence (`LICENSE`): the licence lets you use the code, not the name.

The point is simple: people should always be able to tell the official app from a copy, because a copy could be changed to send their financial data somewhere.

## Forks and copies are welcome, under a different name

If you run, share or publish a changed or unchanged copy under the licence:

- **Use a different name and a different icon.** Don't call it QW Portfolio, or anything that could be mistaken for it (for example "QW Portfolio Plus" or "QWPortfolio").
- **You may say where it came from:** "based on QW Portfolio" or "a fork of QW Portfolio by Quiet Wander", in plain text, without our icon and without suggesting we endorse or support it.
- **Don't use our addresses:** no `quietwander.com` web addresses or email addresses, including the feedback address built into the app. Change the feedback address (`FBM` in `index.html`) to your own or remove it.
- **Keep the copyright notice and the licence,** as the licence requires.

## What's fine without asking

- Writing about, reviewing or linking to QW Portfolio by name.
- Running an unchanged copy for yourself on your own device or network.
- Saying that your project is compatible with QW Portfolio backup files.

## The official app

The only official copy of QW Portfolio is at <https://ppm.quietwander.com>. You can check that it matches the published code: see "Verify the app you are running" in `README.md`.
